Infinite Media — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Infinite Media is a media player designed to play audio files stored locally on your device.

2. Information Collection

Infinite Media is designed to work without an account or registration. The application does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the publisher. Third-party libraries and services, if present, must be assessed separately.

3. Local Media

The application may access audio files on your device to display and play them. Playback is designed to occur locally. The app is not designed to upload your music files to the publisher.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally sell or share personal information. Third-party components must be checked for their own data practices.

5. Data Retention and Security

The publisher does not maintain a user database through the application as designed. Media files remain subject to your device's security and storage settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The application is not designed to collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
